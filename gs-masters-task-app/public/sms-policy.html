About This Program

G.S. Masters, Inc. is a licensed custom home builder in Maylene, Alabama. Our internal construction management system ("GSM Builder") sends operational SMS notifications to our own employees and subcontractors only. This is not a marketing program and is never used to message customers or the general public.

Messages include: morning check-in reminders, end-of-day checkout reminders, daily work log reminders, job dispatch and task assignments, schedule updates, and payment/reimbursement confirmations.

How Workers Opt In

Enrollment is never automatic and never self-service. During onboarding, each worker signs the written consent form shown below. Only after the signed form is on file does a company administrator enter the worker's mobile number into GSM Builder. Signed forms are retained by our office.

SMS Consent Form (as signed by each worker at hire)

I authorize G.S. Masters, Inc. to send SMS text messages to the mobile number I have provided, for work-related notifications including check-in/checkout reminders, daily log reminders, job assignments, schedule changes, and pay/reimbursement confirmations sent from the company's GSM Builder system.

I understand that: message frequency varies (typically 1–5 messages per workday); message and data rates may apply; consent is not a condition of employment or purchase; I may opt out at any time by replying STOP and get help by replying HELP; and my phone number will never be sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Message Frequency & Rates

  • Typically 1–5 messages per recipient per workday. No messages on Sundays except urgent schedule changes.
  • Message and data rates may apply.

Opting Out & Help

  • Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe immediately.
  • Reply START to resubscribe.
  • Reply HELP or call our office at (205) 620-1698 for assistance.

Privacy

Mobile numbers collected for this program are used solely for the operational notifications described above. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.
